01 · The problem

Ride apps assume you read English, have fast data and like filling forms. But people think in landmarks, speak in Hindi, and ride on budget phones over weak networks.

So Haan starts from how people actually say it: “Sadar bazaar le chalo.”

Written into the spec

or less to book, counted from first sign-in

5

90s

in total to find you a rider

02 · Speak

Tap the mic.
Say it your way.

  1. 01

    Speak or type

    Up to 15 seconds of voice, or just type. Hindi, English and Hinglish all work: “Mujhe station jaana hai.”

  2. 02

    It picks out the details

    Pickup, destination, vehicle. If something is unclear it asks one short question in your language, with up to three tap options.

  3. 03

    It reads it back

    Destination, vehicle and fare, aloud and in large text. Two big buttons: Haan or Nahi. Nothing is booked without your Haan.

  4. 04

    Then it finds a rider

    One rider at a time, 15 seconds each. The search starts within 2 km and widens to 5 km after 20 seconds.

03 · Follow

Watch the marker,
not the clock.

The marker glides with its heading instead of jumping. Every status change sends a push notification, even with the app in the background.

  • searching
  • accepted
  • arrived
  • in_progress
  • completed

If the rider’s connection drops you see “Rider connection lost”, and an admin is alerted after 5 minutes.

04 · Fare

₹112

A formula,
not a mystery.

Every fare is max(minimum, base + per km × km + per min × min), rounded to the rupee. Move the sliders; these are the default rates.

The final fare never exceeds the estimate × 1.5 unless an admin overrides it with a note.

07 · For riders

Go online.
Accept. Drive.

A light Rider app for budget phones and weak networks. It survives in the background and never lets motion get between you and Accept.

Documents

Add your name, mobile, vehicle details, licence, RC, selfie and insurance. Auto and Cab also require a permit. Go online after admin approval.

Offers

Full screen, with sound and vibration, even from the background. Pickup distance, trip distance, fare and a 15-second timer. If several riders accept at once, exactly one gets the trip.

Low-data mode

Location every 5 seconds when moving, every 15 when idle, batched on a poor network.

Fare summary

Completed-trip fares for today and this week. Confirm payment in your own cash or UPI records.

08 · Limits

The assistant has
limits. On purpose.

Only five intents exist. Anything else gets one reply: “I can only help with rides.” If it can’t understand you, it says so and opens the map.

book_ridecancel_ridecheck_statushelpunknown

It never

Books, cancels or pays without your on-screen confirmation.

Obeys instructions hidden in what you say or type.

Sees phone numbers, full names, exact home addresses or anyone else’s data. It has no database access.

Gives medical, legal or financial advice, or chats off-topic.

Keeps your audio. Transcripts are text, stored for 30 days, and disclosed in the privacy policy.
